Our story

From social media work to a dedicated studio presence

Meliora Media started as a small operation capturing photos and videos for local retail businesses in and around Malvern. Work shared on Instagram and TikTok developed into repeat business as owners saw the value of professional footage and thoughtful colour grading.

Today the team handles filming, drone operation, editing and voice-over in one creative workflow so that each project keeps a consistent visual direction from concept to final export.
